# Sales — internal working documents

Status: **internal**. Everything here is a working document for whoever sells
Sijilna. The only client-facing artifact in this folder is
[`offer-sheet-ar.md`](offer-sheet-ar.md) — it is the one file that gets printed
and handed to a buyer. The rest never leaves the team.

## What is in this folder

| File | What it is | Who reads it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`pricing.md`](pricing.md) | Single source of truth for packaging and money: the model, the three tiers, add-ons, founding terms, and the market anchors each number is defended with | Anyone who quotes a price |
| [`offer-sheet-ar.md`](offer-sheet-ar.md) | The **client-facing** one-page sheet, in Arabic, print-ready | The buyer |
| [`target-list.md`](target-list.md) | The framework for building the 40-account target list: segments, columns, sourcing, lead scoring, weekly routine. A method, not a list of leads | Whoever builds the list |
| [`discovery-script-ar.md`](discovery-script-ar.md) | The 20-minute first-meeting script in Egyptian colloquial, with the demo path, the price presentation and six objections | Whoever runs the first meeting |

## Operating cadence

These are weekly and monthly quotas, not aspirations. They are the number the
pipeline is planned backwards from.

| Cadence | Quota |
| --- | --- |
| Weekly | **10 new first meetings** booked |
| Weekly | **5 discovery conversations** held (the 20-minute script) |
| Weekly | **2 demos** to a named decision maker with a real quote from their business on screen |
| Monthly | **1 written case study** produced |
| Per closed client | **3 named introductions** requested, by name, at signature or at handover — never "do you know anyone?" |

Three named introductions per closed client is the growth engine: it is the only
channel that produces warm accounts at zero cost, and it is written into the
founding-client terms for that reason.

## Ground rules for anything written here

1. **No claim that is not traceable to `docs/client-guide/_internal/facts.md` or
   `README.md`.** If a feature is not in the digest, it does not go in a sales
   document.
2. **Sijilna is a sales register, not accounting software.** No e-invoicing, no
   tax authority integration, no invoices. There is no such code in this
   repository, so there is nothing to promise. See `pricing.md` §1.
3. **There are zero clients today.** No testimonial, no logo, no reference site
   and no "used by N companies" line exists, and none may be written. Documents
   in this folder are built to work with zero references; the reference section
   is filled after the first client signs.
4. **No contact detail is invented.** A phone number, an email address or a
   company name that nobody supplied stays a `<!-- TODO(المالك) -->` until the
   owner fills it.
5. **The Arabic manual is written, not illustrated.** Describe it as a written
   Arabic guide (17 chapters). Screenshots and video training do not exist yet.

## Where the truth lives

Numbers and packaging: `pricing.md` only. If a price changes, it changes there
first, then in `offer-sheet-ar.md`. Product limits: chapter 16 of the Arabic
manual (`docs/client-guide/ar/16-limits-and-provider.md`) is the honest list of
what the product does not do, and every sales document must agree with it.
