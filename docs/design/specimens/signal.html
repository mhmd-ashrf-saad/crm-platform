Direction C of 3 - proposal

Signal إشارة

The product-marketing direction. Navy is still the anchor and still pinned at its true step, but the middle of the scale is pushed to a vivid steel blue and the neutrals cool off after the paper-light steps, so the screen reads as a modern web app rather than a document. Corners are soft, the secondary button is a soft-filled chip rather than an outline, and the semantic colours carry real chroma.

  • primary-900 #0B3C5D brand navy - the frame: header bar, headings, focus
  • primary-800 #004F7C the action colour - a livelier step of the same family
  • neutral-50 #FAF7F1 brand cream - the light end of the neutral ramp
  • neutral-900 #1F2732 cool ink - body text, after the ramp cools off

Who it suits

Sales floors where the CRM has to sell itself, clients comparing the product to HubSpot or Zoho, and any deployment where the UI is demoed on a projector before it is audited.

What it costs

The richest colour and therefore the highest risk of looking like every other SaaS: it leans on execution. It also drifts furthest from the current brand layer - the button is primary-800 (#004F7C), not the brand navy itself.

Primary scale - derived from #0B3C5D

Eleven steps generated in OKLCH at the navy's own hue (243.9 deg) with one chroma curve, then gamut-mapped into sRGB. #0B3C5D is pinned at primary-900 because that is the step whose lightness it actually occupies - it is not moved to the middle of the scale to look tidy. Same token names in all three directions, different values.

Neutral scale - derived from #FAF7F1

Cream is pinned at neutral-50, which is where its lightness lands (0.977 against Tailwind's 0.985 for gray-50). It is not a stock Tailwind grey: the hue starts at the cream's own 84.6 deg and the ramp is built from there. White is a separate --surface-card token, not a scale step.

Fonts, licensing and self-hosting

The product is installed per client and must not reach a CDN for anything, so every face below is embedded in this file as base64 TTF and is shipped inside the installation. Formats are unsubsetted here (the honest cost of a no-network sheet); a production build would ship WOFF2 subsets.

FamilyLicenceDeliverySource of the licence claim
CairoSIL OFL 1.1self-hostedOFL.txt shipped with the font (checked on this machine)
IBM Plex MonoSIL OFL 1.1self-hostedfonts-ibm-plex (Debian copyright file records OFL-1.1)

Every family named in this sheet is SIL OFL 1.1. That licence allows embedding, redistribution and commercial use, requires the copyright notice and licence text to travel with the font files, and forbids selling the font itself. IBM Plex carries a Reserved Font Name clause: distribute the files unmodified and under their own names.

Proposal, not a decision. This sheet exists so the direction is chosen by looking at it. Nothing here is wired into the product; no file under packages/ was touched. Path: docs/design/specimens/signal.html
