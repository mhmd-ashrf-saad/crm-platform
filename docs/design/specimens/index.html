Sijilna - admin design directions
Proposal - not a decision

Design brief - crm-7qu.1

Three directions for the admin UI. You pick one.

All three derive their primary scale from the brand navy #0B3C5D and their neutral scale from the brand cream #FAF7F1, keep the mark and both wordmarks untouched, and are expressible as the same six token groups (primary, neutral, semantic, type, radii, density) so the token bead can ship whichever one you choose. They differ in character: how deep the navy goes, how warm the paper stays, how loud the semantics are, what the Arabic typeface is, and how sharp or soft the corners are.

Fixed input

Navy #0B3C5D, cream #FAF7F1, the existing mark and wordmarks, and Arabic-first typography. Not up for redesign.

Not a recommendation

No direction in this set is marked recommended or approved. The choice is recorded on the bead once you make it.

No network

Each sheet embeds its real font files and its own CSS. Open it from the filesystem with the network off; nothing is fetched.

Nothing wired in

No application file, no token file and no CSS in the product was changed by this brief.

The decision in plain terms

The three directions are not three shades of the same choice; they answer three different questions.

Is the brand navy the button?

A yes, exactly. B yes, and deeper still. C no - the button is a livelier step of the same family and navy becomes the frame.

Is the product paper or screen?

A paper canvas, white cards. B paper everywhere, hairlines instead of shadows. C white screen, tinted panels.

How much does Arabic shape the type?

A one neutral sans across both scripts. B Naskh for Arabic headings - a printed register. C one geometric family carrying both scripts.

How tight are the grids?

A 52/38 px rows. B 46/34 px. C 56/40 px. Arabic never drops below 16 px in any of them.
